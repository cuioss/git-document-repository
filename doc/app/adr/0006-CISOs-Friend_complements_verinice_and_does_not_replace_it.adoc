== Status

Accepted

== Decision

An ISMS on Git is possible, but a complete ISMS needs catalogues, methodology and reports that established products
have matured over years. verinice is such a product, strong in BSI IT-Grundschutz and ISO 27001.

*The decision:* CISOs-Friend does not compete with a complete ISMS.

* *Core*: SAMM on Git.
* *Extension*: a lean ISMS for development organisations: policies, statement-of-applicability extracts for secure
  development, risks from SAMM gaps, threat models and incident reports.
* *No bridge*: CISOs-Friend delivers nothing into an ISMS product. Where one runs, it refers to a released SAMM
  assessment by its release tag, as any other evidence that lives outside it.

The comparison is in link:../concepts/category-isms.adoc[ISMS and the Comparison with verinice].

== Alternatives rejected

=== A complete ISMS on Git

*Rejected because* it would take years to reach the content verinice ships. The ISO 27001 texts may not be shipped.
Auditors know the established products.

=== A bridge to verinice.veo

Delivering released SAMM assessments to verinice.veo through its REST API, as evidence in the main ISMS.

*Rejected because* it ties the application to the API and object model of one product, and veo offers no field for an
external reference, so an idempotent delivery would need a workaround. A reference to the release tag serves every
ISMS product alike.

=== No ISMS category at all

*Rejected because* the registers and documents of an ISMS fit Git well. The links from SAMM, threat models and incident
reports need a register to point to.

== Consequences

=== Properties of the design

* The scope stays small enough to deliver.
* Organisations with verinice gain a complement, not a competitor.

=== Accepted costs

* Organisations without an ISMS product get no Grundschutz catalogue or certification reports from CISOs-Friend.

=== When this is revisited

* When adopting organisations ask for a full ISMS without a separate product.

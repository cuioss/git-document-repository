The incident-report category as configuration (`categories/incident-reports/`). Reasoning:
link:../concepts/category-incident-reports.adoc[Incident Reports]. Repository: `isms-incidents-<year>` of the year of
awareness.

[#CIFR-IR-1]
== CIFR-IR-1: Report Layout and Generated Output

* An incident report must be stored as `incidents/<id>/incident.yaml`, `narrative/*.adoc` and a generated
  `report.adoc`.
* `report.adoc` must be generated reproducibly from the YAML and the fragments; a CI check must fail when the
  committed `report.adoc` differs from a fresh generation.

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Path |Content

|`incidents/<id>/incident.yaml`
|`occurred-at`, `detected-at`, `aware-at`, `resolved-at`, `category`, `severity`, `personal-data-affected`,
`data-subjects` (categories and approximate numbers of data subjects and records, no person), `nis2-significant`,
`affected-services`, `protection-needs`, `notifications`, `measures` (with issue ids), `timeline`, `tickets` (ids),
`root-causes`

|`incidents/<id>/narrative/*.adoc`
|`course.adoc`, `root-cause-analysis.adoc`, `lessons.adoc`

|`incidents/<id>/report.adoc`
|Generated; never edited
|===

`<id>`: a ULID, the name of the incident's directory; the readable number `INC-<year>-<n>` is assigned on save.
Times: `occurred-at` when the incident began, if known (optional); `detected-at` when it was detected; `aware-at` when
the organisation became aware of it, the start of the GDPR and NIS2 deadlines.

[#CIFR-IR-2]
== CIFR-IR-2: Notification Deadlines

* When personal data are affected, the application must compute a GDPR Art. 33 notification due time of awareness
  plus 72 hours.
* When the report is marked as a significant incident under NIS2 (`nis2-significant`), the application must compute
  an early warning (awareness +24 hours), a notification (awareness +72 hours), every intermediate report an authority
  requested, and the final report one month after the notification was sent. For an incident not resolved by then, a
  progress report meets that deadline instead, and the final report is due one month after resolution.
* The deadline rules must be data of the category's workflow, not code; due times must be shown in the form and in
  the *Due* lens, and a missed due time must stay visible in the released report.

Each row is one entry of `deadlines` in `workflow.yaml`. `when` names a field that must be `true` or present, or
compares two times; the due time is a date field plus a period, and a deadline whose date field is still empty is
`OPEN`; the deadline is `MET` when one of its `met-by` fields holds `sent-at` ≤ due, `MISSED` after due otherwise,
`OPEN` before; a `met-by` entry with `while-unresolved` counts only when `resolved-at` is absent or later than the due
time. A field pointer with `*` yields one deadline per array element.

|`nis2-early-warning`
|`nis2-significant`
|`aware-at` + `PT24H`
|`notifications.nis2-early-warning`

|`nis2-notification`

|`aware-at` + `PT72H`

|`notifications.nis2-intermediate-reports.*`

|`nis2-final-report`

The shipped periods are those of https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj[Directive (EU) 2022/2555],
Art. 23(4); how they read and where a transposition or a trust service provider differs is explained in
link:../concepts/category-incident-reports.adoc#_the_deadline_clock[Incident Reports, The Deadline Clock]. Other
periods are a change of the category's `workflow.yaml`, not of code.

[#CIFR-IR-3]
== CIFR-IR-3: Mandatory Fields

* Saving a draft must be possible with mandatory fields missing; release must be rejected while any mandatory field of
  the schema is missing, with a list of the missing fields.

States: `draft` → `in-review` → `released`; `incident.release` requires step-up. Draft saves validate types only; the
transitions to `in-review` and `released` validate `required`; a failure returns `MANDATORY_FIELDS_MISSING` with the
JSON Pointers of the missing fields.

[#CIFR-IR-4]
== CIFR-IR-4: Affected Services

* Affected services must be picked from the service catalog.
* The application must derive the protection needs of the incident per dimension as the maximum over the effective
  protection needs of the affected services (link:directory.adoc#CIFR-DIR-5[CIFR-DIR-5]).
